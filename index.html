Apostolic Movement & Spirit

The Legion of Mary in Milton

A worldwide lay Catholic association serving the Church under the guidance of Mary Immaculate and the sanction of the ecclesiastical hierarchy.

The Legion of Mary is a lay apostolic association of Catholics who, with the sanction of the Church and under the powerful leadership of Mary Immaculate, Mediatrix of All Graces, serve the Church and their neighboring community on a voluntary basis in about 170 countries.

In Milton, Ontario, Our Lady of Fatima Praesidium unites members from both Holy Rosary Parish and St. Benedict of Nursia Parish. The basic unit of the Legion is called a praesidium, which holds a weekly meeting and allocates substantial apostolic tasks to its members, who always work in pairs under the spiritual direction of our parish clergy.

The Core Aim of the Legion

The object of the Legion of Mary is the glory of God through the holiness of its members developed by prayer and active co-operation in Mary's and the Church's work of advancing the reign of Christ.

Legionary service is grounded in the doctrine of the Mystical Body of Christ: in every person encountered—whether sick, lonely, lapsed, or inquiring—legionaries seek to behold and serve the Person of Jesus Christ through the maternal care of Mary, His Mother.

“
"He who seeks graces without Mary is attempting to fly without wings. True devotion to Mary consists in giving oneself entirely to her, in order to belong entirely to Jesus through her."
— St. Louis-Marie Grignion de Montfort, Inspiration of the Legion Spirit

Key Legionary Principles

  • ✦ Working in Pairs: All apostolic works are carried out two-by-two in the spirit of the Gospel (Luke 10:1).
  • ✦ Strict Confidentiality: Everything discussed during meetings and visits is held under inviolable privacy.
  • ✦ Spiritual Ministry: The Legion avoids political or material relief tasks, focusing purely on spiritual and personal welfare.
  • ✦ Ecclesiastical Obedience: Unreserved loyalty and cooperation with parish priests and the Magisterium.
The Heart of Legionary Life

The Praesidium Altar & Meeting Order

Every Tuesday at 7:00 PM in St. Benedict's Church Nursia Room, members gather around the Altar of Mary for spiritual renewal, accountability, and apostolic mission.

The Official Legion Altar
1
Statue of the Immaculate Conception

The standard model of the Miraculous Medal representation, expressing Mary's maternal mediation and crushing the serpent.

2
The Vexillum Legionis

The Roman military standard baptized for Christ: the Holy Spirit dove above, the oval medallion of Mary, and the globe below.

3
Two Lighted Candles & Fresh Flowers

Signifying living faith, purity of intention, and reverence for the Queen of the Legion during the entire meeting.

4
Pristine White Altar Cloth

Embroidered with the Legion motto: "Legio Mariae", symbolizing the clean table of fraternal charity and prayer.

The Weekly Meeting Order (Every Tuesday 7:00 PM)

Step 1 • 7:00 PM

Opening Prayers & Holy Rosary

Kneeling before the Altar, members invoke the Holy Spirit, recite the Apostles' Creed, and pray 5 decades of the Rosary for our parish intentions.

Step 2 • 7:20 PM

Spiritual Reading & Minutes

Reading from the Legion Handbook or spiritual classic, followed by confirmation of previous minutes and the Treasurer’s statement.

Step 3 • 7:35 PM

Work Reports & Catena

Each pair of active legionaries reports on their assigned apostolic visits. At midway, all stand to recite the Catena Legionis (Magnificat).

Step 4 • 8:05 PM

Allocutio & New Assignments

Spiritual Director or President gives an instructional allocutio. New works are assigned for the week, ending with Concluding Prayers and blessing.

Faith in Action

Our Apostolic Ministries in Milton

Every active member is assigned a weekly spiritual work by the praesidium president, performed in pairs with love, humility, and absolute discretion.

Pilgrim Virgin Statue Visits

Accompanying the blessed statue of Our Lady of Fatima to family homes in Milton for a week stay, introducing or deepening family Rosary devotion.

Homebound & Senior Visitation

Visiting the elderly, sick, and lonely in their homes, Milton retirement residences, and nursing care centers, bringing prayer, holy communion,conversation, and Miraculous Medals.

Welcoming New Parishioners

Assisting our parish priests with outreach to newly registered families and baptismal follow-up, helping newcomers integrate into parish sacramental life.

Auxiliary Member Care

Keeping in regular, warm contact with our Auxiliary (praying) members, providing them with Legion news, annual reunion invitations, and spiritual encouragement.

Catholic Literature & Book Barrow

Distributing Catholic pamphlets, rosaries, Miraculous Medals, and apologetics books at parish events to gently encourage personal inquiry and Catholic faith.

First Saturday & Public Rosaries

Leading and coordinating the First Saturday Rosary of Reparation requested by Our Lady of Fatima for world peace and the conversion of hearts.

Two Paths of Service

Legionary Membership

Every Catholic can join the Legion! Whether called to active field apostolate or the vital power-house of daily intercessory prayer.

Field Apostolate

Active Member

Hands & Feet of Our Lady in Milton
  • ✓ Weekly Meeting: Attend the 75–90 min Praesidium meeting every Tuesday at 7:00 PM at St. Benedict.
  • ✓ Apostolic Work: Perform a definite 2-hour apostolic assignment each week in pairs.
  • ✓ Daily Catena: Recite the Catena Legionis (Magnificat prayer) daily.
  • ✓ Probationary Period: Complete 3 months of accompanied apprenticeship to discern your vocation.
  • ✓ Legionary Promise: Solemn consecration directed to the Holy Spirit under Mary’s standard (18+ years).
Higher Degree: Praetorian Membership

Active members who also commit to: (1) Daily recitation of all Tessera prayers; (2) Daily Holy Mass & Communion; (3) Daily recitation of an approved Office (e.g., Liturgy of the Hours).

Spiritual Powerhouse

Auxiliary Member

The Intercessory Wing of Prayer
  • ✓ Open to Everyone: Perfect for homebound, seniors, busy parents, students, priests, and religious.
  • ✓ No Meeting Requirement: No obligation to attend weekly praesidium meetings or perform field work.
  • ✓ Daily Prayer Service: Pray the entire Tessera prayers daily:
  • • Invocation to the Holy Spirit & Apostles' Creed
    • Five decades of the Holy Rosary
    • The Catena Legionis (Magnificat)
    • Concluding Legion Prayers of Faith
  • ✓ Spiritual Communion: Your daily prayers sustain and empower the active legionaries on their visits!
Higher Degree: Adjutorian Membership

Auxiliary members who, in addition to the daily Rosary and Tessera, attend daily Mass, receive Holy Communion, and recite an approved Divine Office.

Junior Apostolate

Junior Member

Closer to Jesus through Our Lady The Junior Legion of Mary helps young Catholics grow in faith, devotion to Mary, and love for Jesus through prayer, spiritual formation, and service. Juniors participate alongside senior Legionaries in visiting and praying with the sick, elderly, and homebound; bringing the Pilgrim Virgin Statue and Divine Mercy Image to families; and participating in home Enthronement to the Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Immaculate Heart of Mary. They also take part in pen-pal outreach with seniors and seminarians, praying and making Rosaries, distributing Catholic literature and Miraculous Medals, serving at Mass, and helping with parish activities.
  • ✓ Weekly Meeting: Attend the 75–90 min Praesidium meeting every Saturday at 11:45 AM-1.00 PM,Nursia Room, St. Benedict Church.
  • ✓ Apostolic Work: Perform a definite 2-hour apostolic assignment each week in pairs.
  • ✓ Daily Catena: Recite the Catena Legionis (Magnificat prayer) daily.
  • ✓ Eligibilty: Open to Catholic youth ages 8–17

Our Lady in Your Home

The Pilgrim Virgin Home Visitation Program

Invite the Blessed Mother into your household for a blessed week visitation of prayer, peace, and family blessings.

Welcome Our Lady of Fatima

The Pilgrim Virgin Statue program brings a blessed statue of Our Lady of Fatima into Catholic homes in Milton to foster the family Rosary and bring heavenly peace into the domestic church.

How the Home Visitation Works:

  1. Arrival & Enthronement Two active legionaries bring the statue to your home on an agreed date. Together, we recite the welcoming prayers and Rosary.
  2. Daily Family Rosary for a Week The statue resides in a place of honour in your home. Your family is invited to gather each evening for the Rosary and personal intentions.
  3. Farewell & Next Journey Legionaries return on the designated departure day to recite farewell prayers, thanksgiving, and escort Our Lady to the next waiting family.
✦ There is never any cost or fee. This is a complimentary spiritual ministry offered with joy by Our Lady of Fatima Praesidium.

The Legionary Prayer of Faith

Confer, O Lord, on us, who serve beneath the standard of Mary, that fullness of faith in You and trust in her, to which it is given to conquer the world.

Grant us a lively faith, animated by charity, which will enable us to perform all our actions from the motive of pure love of You, and ever to see You and serve You in our neighbor;

A faith, firm and immovable as a rock, through which we shall rest tranquil and steadfast amid the crosses, toils and disappointments of life;

A courageous faith which will inspire us to undertake and carry out without hesitation great things for your glory and for the salvation of souls;

A faith which will be our Legion's Pillar of Fire – to lead us forth united – to kindle everywhere the fires of divine love – to enlighten those who are in darkness and in the shadow of death – to inflame those who are lukewarm – to bring back life to those who are dead in sin; and which will guide our own feet in the way of peace;

So that – the battle of life over – our Legion may reassemble, without the loss of any one, in the kingdom of Your love and glory. Amen.

May the souls of our departed legionaries, and the souls of all the faithful departed, through the mercy of God, rest in peace. Amen.

Causes for Beatification

Legionary Witnesses to Holiness

Three heroic legionaries whose causes for canonization have been introduced in the Church.

Servant of God Frank Duff

1889 – 1980 Founder of the Legion of Mary

Founded the Legion in Dublin, Ireland on September 7, 1921. He served the poorest of the poor and attended the Second Vatican Council as an honored Lay Observer. Cause introduced in 1996.

Venerable Edel Quinn

1907 – 1944 Legion Envoy to East Africa

Despite severe tuberculosis, she traveled thousands of miles across Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda establishing hundreds of praesidia with joyful, heroic charity. Declared Venerable in 1994.

Servant of God Alfie Lambe

1932 – 1959 Legion Envoy to South America

Known as "El Corderito" (The Little Lamb), Alfie planted the Legion across Colombia, Ecuador, Uruguay, Brazil, and Argentina with youthful zeal and humility before dying at age 26.

Liturgical & Legion Year

Annual Feasts & Functions

Special celebrations that unite legionaries in Milton throughout the year.

Late March (Near Annunciation)

The Acies Ceremony

The great annual function where all active and auxiliary members gather to renew their solemn consecration: "I am all yours, my Queen and my Mother, and all that I have is yours."

May 13

Feast of Our Lady of Fatima

Our praesidium's patronal feast day! Celebrated with a parish Rosary, Holy Mass of Thanksgiving, and consecration of our Milton families.

September 7 – 8

Legion Birthday & Nativity of Mary

Commemorating the founding of the Legion of Mary in 1921 on the eve of Our Lady's Nativity, celebrated with prayer and social gathering.

Early December

Annual Praesidium Reunion

A festive social gathering around the Feast of the Immaculate Conception bringing together active and auxiliary members with their families.
