The Acies

The Central Annual Consecration of the Legion of Mary

The Acies is the most important annual ceremony of the Legion of Mary, where active and auxiliary members gather near the Feast of the Annunciation (March 25) to renew their individual and collective consecration to the Blessed Virgin Mary.

The Latin word acies means an army drawn up in battle array, symbolizing legionaries united as a spiritual army under Mary to fight the forces of evil. During the ceremony, members process to the Vexillum—the Legion standard depicting a dove for the Holy Spirit, the inscription “Legio Mariae”, and an oval frame of the Immaculate Conception—and recite the traditional formula:

“I am all yours, my Queen and my Mother, and all that I have is yours.”

Rooted in the total consecration teachings of St. Louis Marie de Montfort, the event renews each member's pledge to work in complete union with and dependence on Mary.

Ceremony Structure

  • Opening Prayers: Recitation of the opening Legion prayers and the Holy Rosary.
  • The Consecration: Members process in single file to touch the Vexillum and pronounce their individual and collective pledge.
  • The Allocutio: A short spiritual talk or reflection given by the Spiritual Director.
  • Mass & Benediction: The ceremony is often followed immediately by the celebration of Holy Mass or Benediction.

The Purpose of the Acies

Celebrated annually near the Feast of the Annunciation, the Acies is a profound renewal of true devotion to Mary in the spirit of St. Louis Marie de Montfort. Founder Frank Duff emphasized in the Legion Handbook that attendance is essential: “The Acies is the great central annual function of the Legion... The essential ideal of the Legion, upon which all else is built, is that of working in union with and in dependence on Mary, its Queen.”

The consecration calls members to hold nothing back—offering all past, present, and future spiritual and material goods to Mary. As De Montfort summarized, the essence of the devotion is surrendering completely to the Most Holy Virgin to belong wholly to Jesus Christ and to perform all actions with, in, by, and for Mary. Full reflections, historical context, and guidance from founder Frank Duff can be explored directly via the Concilium Legionis Mariae source documentation.
